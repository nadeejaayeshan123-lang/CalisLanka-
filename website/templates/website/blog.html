CalisLanka was created with one simple vision - to introduce structured, professional and accessible calisthenics training to Sri Lanka.

THE BEGINNING 2023
01.

From an idea to Sri Lanka's first dedicated calisthenics studio.

CalisLanka began in 2023 from a passion for bodyweight training, movement and helping people discover what their bodies are truly capable of.

What started as [ADD HOW CALISLANKA STARTED HERE] gradually developed into a growing calisthenics community.

The goal has always been bigger than simply creating another fitness centre. CalisLanka aims to create a place where beginners and experienced athletes can develop strength, mobility, discipline and advanced bodyweight skills through professional coaching.

MEET THE COACH

The person behind CalisLanka.

02.

Passion turned into professional coaching.

Thidasa Hettiarachchi is the founder and head coach of CalisLanka. His journey in calisthenics began through a passion for bodyweight strength, movement and mastering skills that require patience, discipline and complete body control.

Over the years, he has developed experience in calisthenics, strength training, mobility and physical conditioning while helping athletes at different experience levels improve their performance.

His coaching philosophy focuses on correct technique, progressive development and building strong foundations before progressing into advanced skills.
